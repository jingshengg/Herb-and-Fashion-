About Us

A regional story, built on trusted traditions

Herbal & Fashion Sdn. Bhd. is a dedicated distributor of premium health and wellness solutions, expanded from its parent company in Singapore. Founded in Singapore in 2005, the brand has built a trusted reputation in modern health supplementation and traditional herbal wisdom. To meet growing regional demand, the company officially expanded to Malaysia in 2011, establishing Herbal & Fashion Sdn. Bhd. to bring high-quality health solutions to the local market.

With the brand's production infrastructure anchored in Singapore, the Malaysian company functions as a dedicated distribution, business development, and regulatory pipeline. It operates as the sole importer and distributor of the premium herbal formulations and Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) nutrition products produced by its parent manufacturing wing. All products distributed by the Malaysian entity are actively manufactured under strict Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) standards in Singapore.

To make these trusted formulations highly accessible, Herbal & Fashion Sdn. Bhd. has strategically grown its local footprint. The company's active distribution network currently covers key regions including Johor and Kuala Lumpur, and has recently expanded its reach to Sarawak. Through this ongoing domestic growth, Herbal & Fashion Sdn. Bhd. aims to provide better services, efficient product delivery, and dedicated health support to customers and practitioners across Malaysia.
